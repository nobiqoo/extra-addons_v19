Odoo Education Management System

A comprehensive yet simplified education ERP software built for all educational institutes.
Odoo Education Management System makes running an institute easy and organized. You can manage student enrollment, attendance, teacher schedules, assignments, grades, and reports all in one place. Role-based access for students, parents, teachers, and admins improves communication, keeps everything connected, and helps create a better learning experience for everyone.

Odoo Education Management System FAQs

Does the system work for different user types?

Yes. Students, parents, teachers, and admins each get their own login and dashboard.

Can I track attendance different ways?

Yes. Mark daily attendance and class attendance with detailed reports.

Can I manage multiple branches of an educational institution?

You can create various branches under your head branch of the school Management system in Odoo and manage the data accordingly. All branches are under the same management, and they can manage them within the same system.

Can I change the grading system?

Yes. Set up your own grade scales, weights, and rules to match your policy.

Does the system handle school transport?

Yes. Manage buses, drivers, routes, pickup spots, and which students ride which bus.

Can students see their school records?

Yes. Students can check grades, attendance, and assignments and download transcripts anytime

How does fee management work?

You can generate fee slips for the particular year, according to your requirement, and set the payment term accordingly.

Is there a notice board?

Yes. Admins and teachers can post news and updates for specific groups or everyone.

How are parent and student portal in Odoo different?

The parent portal allows parents to pay fees, view their children's classes, exams, grades, and switch between multiple profiles if needed, while the student portal in Odoo is designed for students to access their own classes, assignments, and submissions

Can the system handle scholarships?

Yes. Track scholarship forms, who qualifies, approvals, and payments.
